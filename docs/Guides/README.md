# JoInn guides — start here

These pages are written in everyday language. You do **not** need to be a programmer to follow them.

JoInn’s deeper papers (Theory, Plans, Findings) stay as they are for the build work. Come back here whenever something feels cloudy.

## Five-minute picture

JoInn is a **visual app creator**. You zoom a picture of nodes and relationships. Every level looks the same: nodes, joined by relationships that can touch many nodes at once. Open a node and the pieces inside are blocks, snapped where their ports meet. The outermost node is the app. The live engine runs the calculator today. An editor a stranger can snap together, waves, and a native compile are ahead.

Two rules hold the picture. Every join has an opposite that can refuse it or undo it. A thing is true when its past, its present, and its laws agree.

DNA is how JoInn **stores** that graph so it stays robust. It is the research on storage. The screen a person builds on is the picture of blocks and relationships.
